About Us

Mingle & Speak is a modern language academy founded by two passionate language professionals, dedicated to helping learners succeed in real-world communication. We specialize in personalized training in English, French, and Spanish for professionals, students, and exam candidates. Whether you're preparing for a certification, improving workplace communication, or building everyday fluency, our lessons are tailored, practical, and results-focused.

Meet Our Team

Meet Our Founder - Migle Simkute

I'm a passionate English and French trainer with over six years of experience teaching learners of all ages. While I've worked with children, teens, and adults, I now specialize in helping professionals and exam candidates reach their language goals with confidence.

I provide tailored training for certifications such as TOEIC, IELTS, and TCF, and support professionals across industries including aviation, finance, engineering, luxury, and sports. Through my online academy, I offer personalized, goal-oriented lessons that combine practical language use with real-world application.

Meet Our Co-Founder - Dearbhaile Mccusker

With over 5 years of teaching experience, I specialize in both Business and General English for learners of all ages and backgrounds. My journey began with a deep interest in global communication, which evolved into a passion for helping others develop practical language skills for real-world success.

I've worked in diverse multicultural settings, tailoring my lessons to meet the unique goals of professionals, students, and young learners alike. At Mingle & Speak, I focus on delivering dynamic, results-driven lessons that boost fluency, build confidence, and prepare learners for success in international environments.

Courses

Our courses are designed to meet the needs of diverse learners, from beginners to advanced speakers. We offer flexible schedules, online and in-person options, and a variety of resources to support your learning journey.

One-on-One Lessons

  • Personalised learning plan Flexible scheduling
  • Tutors specialise in various areas, including conversational skills, exam preparation (TOEIC, IELTS, TOEFL, Duolingo), business language, and cultural fluency
  • Helps you prepare for professional job interviews
  • French and English for Travel: Prepare for your travels with practical language skills to navigate everyday situations in French – and English-speaking regions

Group Lessons

  • Prepare for your exams (TOEIC, TOEFL, Duolingo, IELTS) in small groups
  • Peer Learning: Benefit from the diverse perspectives and experiences of your peers, enhancing your understanding and retention of the language.
  • Interactive learning: Group settings provide a platform for practising conversational skills in a real-world context.
  • Cost-effective
  • Increased motivation
