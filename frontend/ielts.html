Class 1

IELTS Overview & Strategies

Get a clear understanding of the IELTS exam and what to expect in Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking. We look at the format, question types, timing, common mistakes, exam traps and key strategies.

Class 2

Listening & Reading

Focus on the two receptive skills with practical strategies and exam-style practice. Learn how to approach different question types, manage your time, recognise common traps and avoid losing easy marks.

Class 3

Writing

Focus on Task 1 & Task 2, organisation, structure, vocabulary, grammar and common mistakes, followed by targeted practice and personalised feedback.

Class 4

Speaking

Develop your confidence and accuracy across Speaking Parts 1, 2 & 3. Practise different question types, develop ideas naturally, improve fluency and vocabulary, and avoid common mistakes.

Class 5

Exam Simulation & Feedback

Complete timed exam-style tasks in a realistic setting, followed by detailed personalised feedback. We review your performance, identify remaining weaknesses and give you an approximate band score so you know what still needs work before the real exam.
